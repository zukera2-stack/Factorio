ElectrifiedMainline 0.1.14 — TEST ONLY / ENGINE RAIL SLOT MARKERS

Purpose: read-only visualization of the Factorio engine's rail-signal placement slots
beside custom ВСМ tracks. NO pole snapping, NO placement restriction yet.

1. Install ElectrifiedMainline_0.1.14.zip in Factorio 2.1.20 (remove 0.1.13 ZIP
   to avoid duplicate mod versions; keep a backup of your save).
2. Open a test save with straight, half-diagonal, and both curved ВСМ rails.
3. Point at a ВСМ rail (or stand within 10 tiles) and open console (~).
4. Run: /em-35kv-slots
5. Green rings = out/alternative-out positions; yellow = in/alternative-in.
   Each ring is labeled with index and engine direction; lasts 15 seconds.
6. Screenshot each rail shape, especially curves, and send factorio-current.log
   if any errors appear. Repeat with a real signal beside the same rail.

Important: Factorio console commands can disable achievements on a save. Use
an expendable test save. The diagnostic does not spawn or modify entities,
change rail signals, move poles, or alter Undo/Redo.

Known limitation: the slots belong to signals, not necessarily the ideal
mast foot position. The later mast offset must be determined from screenshots.
