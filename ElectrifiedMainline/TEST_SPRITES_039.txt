Electrified Mainline 0.1.39 - image alignment test

This build modifies ONLY accepted C mast and console images. The rail
connection logic, hidden connector locations, 32 straight / 16 curved / 40
wire reach, catenary dropper dynamics, and Undo/Redo are unchanged.

Source: accepted long mast and simple separate console artwork, cropped
by actual alpha body. Short north built by compressing the same accepted
mast vertically, keeping its width/foundation and NO cut-and-paste seam.
Eight console images are the same accepted console, mirrored as needed.

Screenshot bug from 0.1.38: the console was fitted inside a bbox while
unclean alpha=1 noise of source was counted as the image. Result: console
was much too small and the cable hanger was far from the shaft.

0.1.39:
  * all C masts centered at x=256 of their native 512px sprite frame;
  * contact hanger is registered to per-face Lua anchor pixel positions;
  * right/left console is 132x83 source px, not 70x45;
  * eight 512x384 RGBA facing sprites, scale=0.5, real transparent BG;
  * south 512x512 RGBA, north 512x384 RGBA, native placeable previews;
  * separate icons for south and north items.
