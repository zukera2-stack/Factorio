Version: 0.2.0
  Major Features:
    - First public beta candidate: high-speed rail and a dedicated 25 kV overhead network.
    - Bidirectional 20 MW traction substations and 2 MW electric locomotives.
    - Motor cargo, fluid and battery wagons, each adding 500 kW traction.
    - Finite onboard storage and regenerative braking are standard features.
  Features:
    - Rail replacement planner and configurable stationary charging.
    - English and Russian localization, research and crafting recipes.
  Graphics:
    - Original rolling-stock sprites, pantograph, wagon fans, doors and night headlight.
  Changes:
    - Consolidated motor-wagon power status and removed development test commands.
    - Reduced periodic diagnostic logging.
  Info:
    - Pre-publication candidate based on 0.1.134; no new gameplay changes.
    - Multiplayer, large-factory UPS and final Windows visual acceptance are not confirmed.
