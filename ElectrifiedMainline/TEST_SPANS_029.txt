Electrified Mainline 0.1.29 — LONG SPAN EXPERIMENT
Factorio 2.1.20 build 87512; use a COPY of saved map ВСМ.
Install only ElectrifiedMainline_0.1.29.zip instead of 0.1.28.

SETTINGS: 32 world tiles straight, 16 curved, helper copper reach 40.
The vanilla native copper wire remains the visual conductor; user-approved
mast art, insulators, eight arm offsets, ghosts, rail upgrades and Undo
are unchanged. No new contact/messenger texture is added in this version.
No physical 25 kV voltage or power transfer.

A. STRAIGHT HSR:
1. Place two 25 kV C masts at 30, then 32 tiles along uninterrupted
   custom high-speed straight rails. /em-25kv-status should show a wire.
2. Move a mast beyond 32 tiles: NO contact wire.
3. Remove a CUSTOM ВСМ rail halfway between 32-tile masts: wire
   disappears. Restore custom rail: wire returns.
4. Replace the removed rail by vanilla rail: no wire should reappear.

B. CURVES:
5. On connected custom curved-A, curved-B and half-diagonal rails test
   mast spacings of 10, 14, 16 tiles. Wire should follow accepted span.
6. More than 16 tiles around a turn: NO wire. No skipping across an
   unconnected nearby or parallel railway.
7. Ensure small curved/straight transitions are not left unwired.

C. LIFE CYCLE:
8. Verify robots, blueprint, Undo, Redo, save/load, no orphan helpers,
   and no link from regular power poles.
9. /em-25kv-status prints span limits and counts.
10. Send screenshots and factorio-current.log. If 32-tile span fails,
    report exact mast-to-mast distance and orientation.

Visual appearance remains 0.1.28 native copper; the approved dual
contact/messenger/strut sprite design is a SEPARATE next-stage task.
