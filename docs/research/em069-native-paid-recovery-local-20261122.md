## Owner engineering decision — minimum power 1–60 W (2026-11-22)

Owner decided NOT to pursue ideal/mathematically exact locomotive traction at infinitesimal supplied power. Real electric systems have parasitic currents, capacitances, inrush/starting current and losses; the game mod is an engineering approximation, not a detailed circuit simulator. In the present version the observed **0–60 W deadband is ACCEPTED as a low-power non-goal**, rather than treated as a release-blocking bug. At 1 W and 60 W it is permitted that the train receives 0 J in the tested interval; **this does NOT mean Factorio simulates specific parasitic effects or that 60 W is an actual physical startup-current specification**. The existing `production > 1 J/tick` dispatcher cutoff remains as-is; no speculative source-code change for this alone.

The acceptance criteria **remain strict where they matter**: no locomotive charging or movement from an unpowered, disconnected line when the onboard reserve is empty; source and real EEI buffers may legitimately retain insufficient energy without forced transfer; no fabricated Joules, no double charging, no deletion of already-paid positive-J buffers; under normal operating power (including 1 MW shared by multiple EM locomotives and a real 20 MW source) verify real native demand, power bounds, train movement, fairness, outage recovery, SAVE/LOAD and closed energy ledger to measured numerical tolerance. A black-box zero mismatch in every low-power tick is not an objective. The low-power observation stays documented and reproducible but is **not a blocker by itself**. All unrelated blockers (GUI/Undo/MP, motor wagon 500 kW, regeneration and user acceptance) remain open; no main merge or release approved.
