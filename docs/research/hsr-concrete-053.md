- Vossloh Guiyang–Nanning HSR fastening example: https://www.railwaygazette.com/infrastructure/vossloh-wins-guiyang-nanning-high-speed-line-rail-fastening-contract/57077.article — regularly spaced dark fasteners on pale concrete.
- Rhomberg Sersa IVES: https://germany.rhomberg-sersa.com/de/services/produkte-feste-fahrbahnsystem-ives — prefabricated slabs and black fastening components.
- CRTS III layer/fastening research https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/15/3/1070. Real slab track eliminates loose ballast below the slab in many designs; retained vanilla ballast is intentional owner-defined game adaptation.
