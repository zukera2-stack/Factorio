## Implementation and limits

Real physical locomotive unit_number owns finite storage, zero initially and on old-save migration. Experimental configurable 60 MJ capacity, 2 MW charge, 4 MW discharge per physical locomotive shared across its current native consist. No battery on motor wagons/helpers. Existing grid traction remains first; actual deficit can be supplied by same-train charged locomotives under per-engine and per-store caps, with max-min allocation of available energy across requests. Keep separate 2 MJ real locomotive burner reserve; native helpers one tick. Charge only at standstill, no pending traction for the consist/donor, no same-tick discharge of that consist, valid contact and live auto-import donor; tertiary_input native EEI and same-tick generator-to-charge plan budget after loads. No new energy from plan alone. Conservative freeze when any planned bridge rejected. Rejected route/global freeze may reduce availability; charging while moving/manual import not implemented. Runtime-global setting defaults false; opt-in fixtures use exact engine-generated mod-settings.dat, not runtime QA injection. Startup parameter change clamp counts excess as loss; dynamic setting change and owner GUI not tested. No regen, no efficiency calibration (ideal transfer), no speed writes. New storage history separate from original grid paid cumulative ledger. See architecture/research docs. No claim exact source provenance or universal charging priority under arbitrary topology/source changes, nor 60 UPS.
