# EM087C — design note, NOT implemented / accepted

Goal: event-local topology recomputation without stale rail permissions. Do not simply cache `technical_046.edges` by nearby mast or skip the 600-tick full check. `M.plan` sorts candidate edges globally by distance and IDs and greedily applies degree and triangle constraints; an edit to one candidate can change a later choice beyond the edited pair. Re-running the cheap ordered greedy selection is required, or a separate proof of independence of the entire candidate component.

Proposed separation:

1. **Candidate set:** geometric mast pairs within reach, segregated by surface and force; exact endpoint signature (IDs, names, coordinates, native rail ID). Keep the deterministic tie order.
2. **Physical path certificate:** cache only results of bounded native rail walk, with dependency IDs for every expanded rail and its six neighbor slots (including missing slots). Positive witness alone does not establish that a negative result remains negative. A query can depend on a branch not in its chosen path. Record search-bound and first-pop order. Cache scalar snapshots, no permanent LuaEntity handles.
3. **Invalidation:** on added/removed native HSR rail, collect the rail and its old/new adjacent rails before/after mutation when the API permits; invalidate every certificate that depends on those slots. Mast move/force/surface changes invalidate endpoint signatures. Unknown events, config updates, missing IDs, force merge, surface delete, and load force cold rebuild. Read current validity and physical links before authorizing an edge; never grant based solely on cached event silence. Native `get_connected_rail` is available; `get_rail_segment_rails` also exists but its boundaries include signals/stops, and it does not directly preserve the current bounded search contract.
4. **Full independent verification:** keep existing 60-tick wire audit and 600-tick full topology replan; do not delay validation of a dirty affected corridor. Need an explicit policy for edits by other mods without raised build/destroy events: event-only cache must NOT be enabled until native physical certificate validation and comparison against full solver have been tested. A periodic audit alone would not prove parity for an unrelated dirty event. Use safe fallback to full replan if certificate state is missing or uncertain.
5. **Atomic publication:** only after native links and isolation are confirmed replace the graph used by bridge; blocked dirty areas must not receive unverified new power. No train speed writes or changes to motor lifecycle.

Acceptance tests: compare incremental and full result edge-by-edge after edits on straight/curved/branched/parallel corridors, loop and degree-limit stress, foreign force/surface and no-event modification, native SAVE/LOAD, 100 moving trains with rail changes, 100km line, identical measured energy ledger. Require no new stale-permission interval, and measure p95/p99/max as well as mean. Preserve all 51 previous native scenarios and 256 prototype checks. Windows hidden helper GUI remains separately required. Only merge after explicit owner authorization.
